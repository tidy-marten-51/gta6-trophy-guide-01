    Roadmap to the platinum — Vice Got a Price!

    PowerPyx rates the set 4 out of 10 and about 200 hours: roughly 60–80 hours of story, about 100 hours on the 100% completion checklist, and about 10 hours of cleanup. One playthrough is enough, there are no online trophies, and there is no mission replay — so keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission. The stages below are built from the official descriptions: which chapters and missions, which collectibles and counts, in what order.

    Missable trophies

    Only one trophy is reported as potentially missable. Everything else — including the whole 100% completion checklist — can be finished in free roam after the credits, according to PowerPyx and GTABase.

    Save habits that protect a run

    • Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter, plus one before the final mission. There is no mission replay, so a save is your only way back.
    • Do the Chapter 1 balcony meditation the morning after Down to Earth (see Positive Mental Attitude below) — it is the one reported missable moment.
    • Start stranger and side-character chains as soon as they unlock. One early guide warns their windows can be tied to story progress; PowerPyx notes that missed optional missions do not matter for trophies, since there are far more than the 8 strands Strangers & Freaks needs.
    • Leave cheats off for the whole run: early guides report cheats disable trophy tracking and saving.
